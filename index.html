About Student Tool

Welcome to Student Tool, a website designed to make studying easier and more organized.

Our goal is to provide students with simple, free tools that help with daily study activities.

What you can do here

  • Use a focus timer for study sessions.
  • Organize assignments and study tasks.
  • Calculate grades and percentages.
  • Use a basic calculator.
  • Write and download study notes.
  • Create flashcards for revision.

We aim to keep the website simple, useful, and accessible. We welcome suggestions that can improve the experience for students.
